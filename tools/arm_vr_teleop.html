Controllers

Grip (middle finger)Hold = this controller takes over its arm; release = that arm stops in place
Trigger (index finger)Drives the gripper while the grip is held: fully pressed = closed, released = open

Bare hands (hand tracking)

FistCurl the middle, ring and little finger into the palm and hold for a quarter of a second = take over; open these three fingers = that arm stops in place. The arm follows your wrist
Thumb + index fingerDrive the gripper while the fist is held: fingertips together = closed, 8 cm apart or more = fully open, proportional in between

Enable hand tracking in the headset settings and put the controllers down; the headset switches to hands by itself (a controller on one side and a bare hand on the other is fine). Keep your hands where the headset can see them (in front of you): a hand that leaves the view is released automatically. Do not pinch thumb and index finger with the palm facing your face: that is the headset's system gesture (the left hand exits, the right hand opens the system menu). Bare hands get no vibration; a tone marks take-over, release and a blocked move.

Cube colours

Cube on the controller / wrist: grey = idle · green = engaged · red = would collide, holding · orange = tracking lost, release the grip (open the hand) and try again · blue = moving to a preset pose · purple = teleop server not responding. With bare hands every finger joint also gets a small cube (to judge how well the hand is tracked); the thumb and index fingertips turn white while the gripper follows the pinch.

Before entering, stand in the stance selected when the teleop server was started and look straight ahead (directions are fixed by the heading of the headset at the moment you enter); if they are off, long-press the Meta button to recenter. Out of reach? Release the grip (open the hand), bring your hand back and grip again (make a fist again).

No headset: simulated controller

While the grip is held: W/S forward / back · A/D left / right · E/Q up / down (1 cm per press) · Z/C turn 5° about the vertical axis
