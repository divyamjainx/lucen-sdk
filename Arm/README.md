# Arm

A 6-DoF arm with a gripper (6+1 DoF) on a fixed base, driven by teleoperation or by joint position targets
through the bridge. It is used alone, or as a **leader–follower pair** in which you move one arm by hand and
the other copies it.

How to operate it — front ends, first enable, gravity feed-forward, calibration — is in
[`docs/arm_guide.md`](../docs/arm_guide.md). This page holds what is specific to this robot.

## Configurations

| Config | Setup | Joint names | Bridge port |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Arm_pair.yaml` | Leader + follower on one MC02 board: follower on CAN1, leader on CAN2 | `follower_joint1` … `follower_gripper_joint`, `leader_joint1` … `leader_gripper_joint` | 9881 |
| `Arm.yaml` | One arm on the board (CAN1) | `joint1` … `gripper_joint` | 9880 |

`Arm.yaml` ships **uncalibrated** (`calibrated: false`, zero offsets, gravity feed-forward off): calibrate it
on your arm before the bridge will enable the motors (arm guide, section 5).

## Quick start

```bash
# simulation
python tools/fake_bridge.py --config Arm/Arm_pair.yaml --grav-scale 1.0 --view     # terminal 1 (mjpython on macOS)
python tools/arm_lead_follow.py --config Arm/Arm_pair.yaml                          # terminal 2: in the window, double-click the leader's hand and Ctrl+right-drag

# robot
python bridge.py --config Arm/Arm_pair.yaml                                         # on the robot computer
python tools/arm_lead_follow.py --config Arm/Arm_pair.yaml                          # same machine, or --host <robot-ip>
```

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `Arm.yaml`, `Arm_pair.yaml` | Configuration: joint table, motor parameters, gains, poses, gravity feed-forward, bridge port |
| `urdf/arm.urdf` | URDF (one arm) |
| `mjcf/arm.xml` | MuJoCo model as exported from CAD (one arm) |
| `mjcf/arm_scene.xml`, `mjcf/arm_pair_scene.xml` | Working scenes used by the tools; generated by `tools/build_arm_model.py` — do not edit by hand |
| `meshes/` | STL meshes, simplified for display and collision. Mass, centre of mass and inertia come from the URDF / MJCF, not from the meshes |

In `arm_pair_scene.xml` the two bases are placed side by side one metre apart. This placement is only a
default; it affects the collision pre-check between the two arms, not the gravity feed-forward.

## Joints

| Joint | Function | Range (rad) | Single / follower id | Leader id | Motor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `joint1` | Base yaw | ±2.356 | 1 | 8 | RS06 |
| `joint2` | Shoulder pitch (zero is at the lower limit) | 0 … 3.142; teleoperation is limited to 2.9 | 2 | 9 | RS06 |
| `joint3` | Elbow (zero is at the lower limit) | 0 … 2.618 | 3 | 10 | RS06 |
| `joint4` | Wrist pitch | ±1.571 | 4 | 11 | RS02 |
| `joint5` | Wrist yaw | ±1.571 | 5 | 12 | RS00 |
| `joint6` | Wrist roll | ±2.094 | 6 | 13 | RS00 |
| `gripper_joint` | Gripper: 0 = fully open, 1.955 = closed; each finger travels 43 mm | 0 … 1.955 | 7 | 14 | RS05 |

Motor peak / rated torque: RS06 36 / 11 N·m, RS02 17 / 6 N·m, RS00 14 / 5 N·m, RS05 (gripper) 5.5 / 1.6 N·m.

## Poses

- **Zero pose (`home_pose`) = stowed pose**: upper arm lying flat on its lower limit, forearm folded back on
  top of it (also on its lower limit), wrist resting on the upper arm in line with the forearm, gripper fully
  open. Gravity presses joint2 and joint3 into their stops, so the arm rests here with the power off. This is
  the pose used for calibration. For joint1, joint5 and joint6 — which gravity does not locate — centre the
  base in its travel, keep the gripper pointing straight and its fingers level; **on a pair, set these three
  joints identically on both arms**, or the follower's hand will sit at a fixed angle to the leader's.
- **Ready pose (`ready_pose`)**: joint2 1.3, joint3 2.17, joint4 −0.87 — upper arm near vertical, forearm
  pointing forward and up, gripper level.

To look at the model: `python -m mujoco.viewer --mjcf=Arm/mjcf/arm_scene.xml` (`mjpython` on macOS).

## Gravity feed-forward

`Arm_pair.yaml` ships with `gravity_ff.scale: 0.53`. Confirm it on your arms before relying on it (arm guide,
step 3): start the bridge with a lower `--grav-scale`, raise the forearm with the sliders, and work upward.
Lead by hand reports how much to add in its status line. With too little feed-forward the leader feels heavy
and sags; with too much the arms rise by themselves.

## Leader–follower

Both arms are powered. The leader is not limp: it holds itself with position control and gravity
feed-forward, and follows your hand once you push it past a small threshold; let go and it stays where it is.
The follower copies the leader's measured joint angles, rate-limited by the safety profile, and its gripper
follows the leader's gripper.

Keys: `l` / `f` choose which arm you lead · `h` both arms home · `y` ready · `g g` gravity calibration ·
`o` / `c` gripper open / close · space lock / unlock · `x` quit · Ctrl+C emergency stop.

## Known collision

The bridge does not check for self-collision. With the forearm still folded, raising the upper arm (joint2)
past vertical drives the wrist into the base. Unfold the forearm first, then raise the upper arm.
